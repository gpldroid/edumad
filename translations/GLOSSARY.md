## Rules
- Never translate all uses of «العبودية» as a legal finding; retain the distinction between literal legal slavery and the author's explicitly metaphorical or analytical use.
- Use the legally established term in the relevant jurisdiction and explain differences in notes where needed.
- Keep one preferred term per concept throughout each edition; record justified exceptions.
- Do not collapse trafficking, smuggling, forced labour, and exploitation into synonyms.
